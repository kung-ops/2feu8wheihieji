Apps that come with APK Sandbox's virtual Android phone
=========================================================

LineageCalculator.apk   Calculator (com.android.calculator2), Android 9
LineageEasterEgg.apk    Android Easter Egg (com.android.egg), Android 9

Both are unmodified builds taken from LineageOS 16.0
(lineage-16_0-20180828-UNOFFICIAL-klte). They are built from the Android
Open Source Project's packages/apps/ExactCalculator and
frameworks/base/packages/EasterEgg.
